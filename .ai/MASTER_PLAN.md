# Master Plan

The MVP progresses through: (1) execution/documentation baseline, (2)
foundation and local runtime, (3) tenant-aware identity and directory, (4)
ticket lifecycle, (5) conversation/audit, (6) media, (7) requester and staff
interfaces, (8) AI gateway and text analysis, (9) AI review/response, (10)
voice/jobs, (11) search/rating/dashboard, and (12) hardening/release.

Each phase is represented by one independently verified Goal in
`EXECUTION_PLAN.md`; no Goal begins automatically after its predecessor.
