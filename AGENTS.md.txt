Every Goal is local, but every decision must remain globally consistent
with PROJECT.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, BUSINESS_RULES.md, and approved decisions.

Before starting a Goal, reload the project context from the repository.

After completing a Goal, persist all information required for the next Goal
inside the repository.

Never rely on conversation history for continuity.

One execution = One Goal.

A Goal is the largest unit of implementation.

Never implement two Goals in the same execution.

Finish the current Goal completely.

Update all documentation.

Prepare the next Goal.

Stop and wait.
